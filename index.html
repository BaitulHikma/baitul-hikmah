Phone Notifications

Setup alarms, sounds & instant notifications for your device.

1
Allow Android Notifications
Checking status...

⚠️ Notifications are currently blocked in Chrome:

  1. Tap the 🔒 Lock icon or ⚙️ Tune icon on the address bar above.
  2. Tap Permissions → set Notifications to Allow.
  3. Or open phone Settings → Apps → Chrome → Notifications → Turn On.
2
Instant Delivery (Like Messenger)
To get instant alarms even when your phone screen is locked:
  1. Go to Android Settings → Apps → Chrome (or Baitul Hikmah).
  2. Tap Battery → select "Unrestricted" (prevents phone from sleeping the alerts).
3
Add App to Home Screen
Tap Chrome's 3 dots (⋮) → tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" for the best full-screen experience.
1
Add to Home Screen (Required for iOS)
Apple iOS only delivers lockscreen notifications when added to your Home Screen:
  1. In Safari, tap the Share icon (⎘ square with arrow at the bottom).
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen (+) → tap Add.
2
Enable Notifications on iPhone
Open Baitul Hikmah from your home screen and tap the notification button to enable alerts.
3
iPhone Settings Check
Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → Baitul Hikmah → ensure Allow Notifications, Sounds, and Badges are switched ON.
1
Browser Notification Permission
Click below to allow browser popups and sound alarms.
2
Focus & Do Not Disturb
Ensure Windows "Focus assist" or macOS "Do Not Disturb" is turned off so alert banners pop up.
